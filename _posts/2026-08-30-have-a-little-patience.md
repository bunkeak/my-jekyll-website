The change those two months was about patience and how we can change our feelings while trying to be patient. First image shows the results of the little one and me from her birthday party. We needed to wait quite a bit for them to be ready. The patience was worth it in my opinion.

{% include image.html file="20260725_115700.jpg" alt="Four pieces of home painted ceramic results of the Birthday Party." caption="Our self-painted plates and cups." %}

We had a wedding in July of my brother, too. Just some days before that, I broke a part of my foot and needed to slow down significantly. I was really not used to this, so this was my lesson over the two months. Get used to new conditions and stay happy and get healthy again. End of July was the end of the first school of the little one, or shall I name her tall one now? The purple or pink one quite in the center is from her, we are very proud of her.

{% include image.html file="PXL_20260731_072546802.jpg" alt="Self-painted pickets of the class of the tall one." caption="End of school ritual." %}

We stayed a bit longer there and on the first evening, first time experiencing falling tide for the tall one, we took this image.
